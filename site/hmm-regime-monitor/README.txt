The monitor is long-only in the position-direction sense: it never recommends
short positions. Swing horizons (20 and 63 trading days) and long-term horizons
(126 and 252 trading days) are both first-class outputs. Suggested actions are
research and risk-review prompts, not automated trading instructions.
